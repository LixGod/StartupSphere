# Startup Sphere - Beginner's Business Guide

## 📊 Dashboard Overview - How It Works

### The 4 Main Numbers

**1. 📦 Total Products**
- How many different items you have in inventory
- Example: You sell 5 different phone cases = 5 products

**2. 🛒 Total Orders**
- How many times customers bought from you
- Example: 10 customers each bought 1 item = 10 orders

**3. 💰 Total Money In (Revenue)**
- All money you received from sales
- Example: 10 orders × ₹1000 each = ₹10,000 revenue

**4. 📊 Net Profit**
- Money left after paying all expenses
- Formula: Revenue - Expenses = Profit
- Example: ₹10,000 (income) - ₹3,000 (costs) = ₹7,000 (profit)

---

## 📈 This Month's Numbers

Every section shows:
- **Income**: Money from all orders this month

## 💳 Invoices & Sales - How They Connect

### What is an Invoice?
An invoice is a **bill** you give to customers showing:
- What they ordered
- How much they paid
- When they paid

### The Relationship

```
Customer Buys (Sales Order) → You Create Invoice → Payment Received
```

### How It Works in Your App

1. **Create a Sale** (In Sales section)
   - Customer buys 5 products
   - System records the order
   - Total amount: ₹5,000

2. **Create Invoice** (In Accounting section)
   - Based on that sale
   - Shows itemized list
   - Includes taxes (GST)
   - Customer reference number

3. **Money Comes In**
   - Once customer pays the invoice
   - Money gets added to your revenue
   - Shows on your dashboard

### Example Flow

```
Sales Order Created
├─ Customer: Rajesh Kumar
├─ Products: 2 × Phone Cases (₹500 each) = ₹1,000
├─ Date: Dec 20, 2025
└─ Status: Pending Payment

    ↓

Invoice Generated
├─ Invoice #INV-001
├─ Subtotal: ₹1,000
├─ GST (18%): ₹180
├─ Total Due: ₹1,180
└─ Due Date: Dec 27, 2025

    ↓

Customer Pays
├─ Amount: ₹1,180
├─ Date Received: Dec 21, 2025
└─ Status: Paid ✓
```

## 💡 Key Business Terms Explained

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Revenue** | Total money coming in | Your orders total ₹10,000 |
| **Expense** | Money you spend | You pay ₹3,000 in salaries |
| **Profit** | Money left after expenses | ₹10,000 - ₹3,000 = ₹7,000 |
| **Order** | A customer's purchase | Customer buys 3 items |
| **Invoice** | Bill sent to customer | Document showing what they owe |
| **GST** | Tax added to invoice | 18% on ₹1,000 = ₹180 |
| **Net Profit** | Your actual profit | Profit margin shows % |

## 🎯 Tips for Beginners

### 1. **Track Everything**
   - Record every sale
   - Record every expense
   - Your dashboard will show accurate numbers

### 2. **Invoice When You Sell**
   - Create invoice immediately after sale
   - Send to customer
   - Shows you're professional

### 3. **Check Monthly Profit**
   - Look at "This Month's Profit"
   - If negative, you're losing money
   - If positive, you're making money

### 4. **Keep Profit Margin Healthy**
   - Good profit margin: 20-40%
   - Your dashboard shows the %
   - Try to keep expenses low

### 5. **Monitor Inventory**
   - Check "Low Stock Alerts"
   - Reorder before items run out
   - Don't overspend on inventory

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Do I need an invoice for every sale?**
A: Yes! It's professional and helps track payments.

**Q: What if a customer doesn't pay?**
A: Mark it in your notes, follow up with them, then delete the invoice if they decide not to buy.

**Q: What expenses should I track?**
A: Everything - salaries, rent, supplies, shipping, utilities, etc.

**Q: How often should I check my profit?**
A: Weekly! It helps you make better business decisions.

**Q: What's a good profit?**
A: Aim for at least 20% profit margin. Higher is better!
